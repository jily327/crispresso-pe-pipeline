# Scientific measurements in the 1.1.0 candidate

## Prime editing

Enter the experimental spacer and extension and the WT and intended amplicons in their supplied orientation. Setup shows which strand contains a unique guide and whether the extension maps. A qualified reverse-strand copy uses the reverse complements of its supplied WT and intended reference inside the backend. A point at zero-based coordinate `p` maps to `length − 1 − p` on the other strand. Ranges are transformed at both ends. Saved evidence includes original and effective references, the original guide and the coordinate transform. Competing placements remain blocked.

Check the predicted window in Setup and actual `Reference.include_idxs` in Results. The latter determines which changes enter a window-based native measurement. Whole-product identity is assessed over the complete amplicon. Explicit windows are preserved in original reference coordinates. For a secondary copy bearing an extension-region polymorphism, the supplementary allele measurement records WT, exact intended product retaining the SNP, exact intended product with the RTT nucleotide converted, other and unresolved. Its population is independently routed by evidence outside that SNP. [Normalization and native allele qualification](validation/pe-normalization/README.md)

## Physical read orientation and reconstruction

Analysis trim settings count leading bases separately on physical R1 and R2 after demultiplexing. A barcode orientation setting determines barcode extraction. To establish amplicon strand, exact reconstruction and copy routing examine strand-specific words shared between the WT and intended products, require agreement between the two physical mates and retain their original identifiers. Sequence reversal includes reversal of its corresponding quality positions when evaluating base evidence. Missing strand evidence, periodic overlaps and competing product boundaries are explicit exclusions. Terminal read-through requires unique whole-template support. A different overlap requirement across WT and PE or across eligible copies can invalidate an otherwise nonempty exact denominator.

The canonical exact PE fraction is **exact PE / (exact WT + exact PE)**. A partial edit, a full-length molecule carrying extra changes or an ambiguous reconstruction contributes to neither count. Read-pair exact products, aligned copy products, family-tolerance estimates and native CRISPResso PE-reference fractions each retain their own evidence and eligibility rules.

## Two substitutions

For a two-substitution design, the normal workflow writes `metrics/<sample>/<target>/intended_sites.json`, its TSV and an offline HTML table. Results, QC and Excel expose the same stored counts. Sites A and B follow ascending coordinates on each supplied reference. When reversing the presentation strand, map coordinates and swap A-only and B-only labels when comparing the same physical substitutions. Intended-state correspondence requires uniquely oriented homologous flanks across eligible copies. Scientific gates on copy routing remain effective when a joint target state can be established independently.

| Measurement | Numerator | Denominator |
|---|---|---|
| Paired Q30, single site A or B | Concordant intended base at the named site | Pairs with unique, matching WT or edited local context in both mates and Q30 or better at that site in both |
| Paired Q30, joint haplotype | Pairs concordantly called WT/WT, A-only, B-only or AB, as named | Pairs with a concordant two-site state and qualifying quality at both sites in both mates |
| Any intended site, paired Q30 | A-only + B-only + AB | Same joint population |
| Four full-length templates | Exact pair reconstruction of the named complete template | Exact reconstruction as any of the four templates |
| CRISPResso aligned site A or B | Intended base in allele rows at the named site, weighted by integer read abundance | Ordinary alleles with an observable call at that site under the specified placement contract |
| CRISPResso joint sites | Abundance of the named two-site outcome | Ordinary alleles observable at both sites, including observed other/mixed states under the recorded method |

The paired caller requires 12 local bases on either side of the site or site pair. Its single-site denominator can exceed the joint denominator. A noncanonical local base can remove a read from the paired context assay. A change outside the Q30 context can leave its site call intact. Full-length template matching excludes additional changes anywhere. The backend allele report retains other bases, additional substitutions/indels, incomplete coverage and alignment ambiguity. Its underlying integer counts allow these populations to be audited independently.

Each physical pair enters the combined group once. For paired Q30 copy attribution, two disjoint 15-base regions flanking the edits must distinguish the copies independently of the editing event and agree in both reads. The workflow selects validated markers from the supplied panel and records their coordinates. With no qualifying markers, the paired counts stay in the unresolved partition. Full-template identity can establish a copy if the complete template is unique in the panel. A CRISPResso per-reference alignment tally has a different population from the biological copy assignment. A denominator of zero yields an unavailable fraction.

The separate `peanalyzer haplotypes` command measures a verified demultiplexed archive from a WT/intended reference FASTA when original guide metadata are unavailable. Its arguments specify the two analysis trims, optional independent copy marker starts and declared negative controls. It does not run a native PE experiment. [Real EPSPS source audit and reproduction command](validation/epsps-real/README.md)

## Nuclease, controls and biological statistics

Choose `indel_nuclease` and enter each amplified copy and its guide/window definition separately. Window positions refer to the actual WT sequence of that copy. A primary W2 analysis and a matched exploratory W3 analysis report their separate counts. For multi-copy results, inspect the combined, confidently assigned, ambiguous and unassessable populations together. The combined numerator counts affected physical molecules in the selected window.

Declare negative controls in sample setup or in the `sample_role` column. A verified empty declared control remains in the inventory, gets an explicit QC record and supplies no percentage or statistical observation with value zero. An unexpected empty experimental sample stops the workflow. Inspect recorded bridge matching, possible trim-boundary shifts, prepared read counts and the configured depth assessment alongside each scientific denominator. Fixed trimming remains separately recorded.

Biological summaries report the unweighted mean of eligible biological fractions and sample SD using n−1. With fewer than two reportable biological replicates, the SD cell is unavailable. Pooled read-weighted fractions retain separate numerator and denominator fields. The configured minimum for inference defaults to two biological replicates per arm or two complete paired blocks and can be raised. [Statistical contracts](m5-statistics.md)

## Migration and verification

Open existing workspaces in place for read-only verification. New analyses write into a new workspace. Historical plans retain their stored routing, metric, statistical and reporting contracts. To apply a new measurement or sample role, start a fresh analysis from the source files and maintain both result paths. A current statistics/export view cannot restore unavailable historical reagents or certify historical experimental sample assignments.

The Windows launcher chooses a version-specific output folder. Keep the historical release, its scientific workspaces and installation-scoped credentials available while qualifying the candidate. File verification checks the export bytes and their source binding. Scientific verification also checks the original inputs, workflow and backend evidence.
